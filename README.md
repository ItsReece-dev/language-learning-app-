In this project, I’m building a mobile-first AI-powered language learning app designed specifically for spoken Mandarin. My goal is to create an interactive learning experience that combines structured lessons, listening and speaking exercises, and realistic AI-powered conversations.

Users will be able to create an account through passwordless magic-link authentication and complete a personalized onboarding process based on their Mandarin level, learning goals, and interests. From there, they can work through a structured curriculum consisting of 12 chapters and 86 lessons while tracking their progress over time.

A major focus of the application is practical speaking experience. Users will be able to record themselves speaking Mandarin, receive AI-powered transcriptions, and practise their pronunciation. I’m also implementing real-time AI roleplay conversations where users can practise situations such as ordering food, travelling, shopping, meeting new people, or handling everyday conversations.

Premium users will have access to additional functionality, including the ability to generate their own custom AI conversation scenarios based on situations they specifically want to practise.

The application will also track statistics such as speaking time, listening time, completed lessons, and overall learning progress.

For the technical side of the project, I’m using Expo and React Native with TypeScript and Expo Router for the mobile application. Supabase will handle authentication, PostgreSQL data storage, and Edge Functions, while OpenRouter will provide access to the language models powering the AI features. Expo's audio and speech functionality will be used for voice recording, playback, and speaking exercises.

Key functionality includes:

* Cross-platform support for iOS and Android using Expo and React Native
* Passwordless authentication through Supabase magic links
* Personalized onboarding based on level, motivation, and interests
* A structured Mandarin curriculum with 12 chapters and 86 lessons
* Dedicated listening and speaking practice
* Voice recording and AI transcription for pronunciation exercises
* Real-time AI-powered Mandarin roleplay conversations
* Custom AI-generated conversation scenarios
* Speaking, listening, and lesson progress tracking
* Premium features and an integrated in-app paywall
